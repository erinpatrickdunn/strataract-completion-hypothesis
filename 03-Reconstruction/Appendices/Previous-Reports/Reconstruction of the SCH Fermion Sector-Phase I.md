## Part VIII — Reconstruction Conclusions

This reconstruction establishes:

1. **An explicit A→B→C derivation exists for one construction.** Part III documents, in full, the route from $S_{\rm geo}$ through exact bosonization to a one-fermion-loop effective potential, with every approximation and truncation labeled.

5. **The reconstruction does not determine whether another construction reproduces Appendix P.** No alternative A→B→C chain (large-$N$, CJT/2PI, or otherwise) has been attempted. Whether such a chain would yield Theorem 6's or §P.11's stated forms is unknown.

6. **Revision of SCH has intentionally not begun.** Per the Constitution's own Phase I/Phase II distinction (stated at the outset and preserved throughout): this document tests $S_{\rm geo}$'s consequences under the frozen action.
