## Comparability Protocol (adopted, governs Path 2)

Before any comparison between the Target-0 object and a new construction is attempted, three questions must be answered *for each* object independently, in writing, before they are set side by side:

1. **What object does this construction compute?** (Not "the effective potential" generically — the specific functional, at the specific loop order, under the specific field restrictions.)
2. **What approximations or definitions are built in?** (Per the Scope Principle: stated as constitutive definitions where that's what they are, per C2's finding about Target 0 — not retroactively described as "approximations" if they were actually scope choices.)
3. **What is the overlapping domain, if any, where comparison is licensed?** This must be established before any disagreement or agreement between the two is interpreted as physics. Two objects can fail to agree simply because they compute different things, on different domains, under different definitions — that would be a null result about scope mismatch, not a discovery about $S_{\rm geo}$.
