# Track-B: Peer Review, Audit, & Adjudication Pipeline

This directory acts as the active formal workspace for internal technical audits, convention alignment logs, and consensus-driven error corrections within the Strataract Completion Hypothesis (SCH) framework.

## Purpose & Structural Role
Track-B is the mechanism that ensures the integrity of the canonical suite. Whenever a critical sign-convention sensitivity, coefficient error, or foundational physical paradox is identified within an active document, it is isolated here for rigorous tracking and peer evaluation. 

## Pipeline Mechanics
* **`unresolved/`** Contains open, active technical investigations, mathematical inquiries, or conflicting derivation attempts that are currently undergoing scrutiny. No downstream canonical work may depend on materials resting within this state.
* **`resolved/`** Houses the complete, permanent audit logs, resolution packages, and independent confirmation reports generated once a consensus or correction has been mathematically settled and verified. These documents stand as the authoritative record of why an error occurred and exactly how it was corrected.
