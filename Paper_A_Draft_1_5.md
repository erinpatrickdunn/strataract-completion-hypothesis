**1. The Anomaly Cluster: A Common Structural Signature**

**1.1 Galactic Rotation Curves**

Stars at the outer edges of spiral galaxies orbit at velocities
incompatible with the gravitational influence of visible mass alone. The
anomaly is strongest in dispersion-dominated systems and weakest or
absent in coherently rotating fast-rotator spirals and globular
clusters. The Milky Way exhibits observed rotation velocities of
approximately 220 km/s at 50 kpc against a Newtonian prediction of
approximately 100 km/s from visible mass. NGC 3198, NGC 6503, and
Andromeda show similar discrepancy factors of three to five times
visible mass.

**1.2 Elliptical Morphology-Lensing Correlation**

correlation is a direct prediction of any framework in which geometric
organizational state contributes independently to gravitational
sourcing. Standard particle dark matter models have no mechanism to
produce shape-dependence, since dark matter distribution is governed by
mass, not morphology.

The JWST anomaly is explained within this prediction. Within any given

**C.4 The Sympathetic Nucleation Mechanism**

The sympathetic nucleation mechanism operates identically within any
given cycle regardless of which result PT-1 establishes. The
within-cycle matter surplus is a consequence of the condensate
structure, not of the meta-historical alternation pattern. Standard
vacuum pair creation produces a matter-antiparticle pair with opposite
chirality -- the default outcome, net baryon number zero. In the
presence of a background condensate with net chirality \<A^0\> not equal
to 0, two qualitatively different events are available:

> Event Type 1 (standard): particle + antiparticle, opposite chirality.
> Net baryon number: 0.
>
> Event Type 2 (sympathetic): two particles, same chirality, aligned
> with condensate. Net baryon number: +2.

The probability ratio is estimated dimensionally as P(Type 2) /
P(Type 1) ~ \|\<A^0\>\|^2 / m_eff^2. This is small when condensate
chirality is weak but nonzero whenever \<A^0\> is not equal to 0. Under
the theoretical prediction (C.2), \<A^0\> inverts at each bounce, so the
Type 2 rate is positive in matter cycles and the equivalent
antimatter-generating rate is positive in antimatter cycles. Under the
logical foil (C.3), \<A^0\> grows across cycles, so the Type 2 rate
compounds. Both predict strong matter domination within any given cycle;
they differ only in the meta-historical balance.

**C.5 Relationship to the Sakharov Conditions**

baryon number violation via Event Type 2 nucleation; CP violation via
the net chirality of the condensate vacuum \<A^0\> not equal to 0;

non-equilibrium event by construction. These are structural
correspondences, not derivations -- the formal demonstration that each
Sakharov condition is quantitatively satisfied requires the Bogoliubov
analysis of Proof Target PT-2 and the FLRW reduction of Calculational
Target viii. The structural correspondence holds regardless of which
version of the chirality conjecture PT-1 establishes.

**C.7 Identified Proof Targets**

The following calculations are required to formally establish the
theoretical prediction and close the open proof targets.

**PT-1 \[Discriminating\]:** Action of the antipodal map on the spinor

Demonstration that baryon number violation (Type 2 nucleation), CP

the logical foil (C.3).

PT-1 is confirmatory, not discriminating. The theory has a prediction:
chirality inverts at each bounce, successive cycles alternate, and the
standard spin representation on S3 is the basis for that prediction. The
formal proof is outstanding. These are different things. The framework
is not in an undecided state pending PT-1 -- it has a stated theoretical
position and an identified proof target for that position.
