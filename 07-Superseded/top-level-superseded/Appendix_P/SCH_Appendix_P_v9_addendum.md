**CT-xix** [New in v9]. Antipodal condensate coupling and global mode contribution. Formally derive the mechanism by which condensate waves propagating across $S^3$ couple into global rotational modes at their antipodal convergence points, and establish the resulting energy transfer rate from local black hole emission to global $S^3$ mode excitation.

*Physical background.* On $S^3$, every geodesic departing from a point converges at the unique antipodal point. A condensate wave emitted by a black hole therefore has a geometric destination: the antipodal point at which the full wave amplitude arrives rather than dispersing across an unbounded volume. At the antipodal point, the nonlinear quartic self-coupling $\lambda$ in $S_{\text{geo}}$ acts on the converging wave amplitude and in principle converts arriving wave energy into excitation of global $S^3$ modes — the lowest-frequency eigenstates of the condensate field on the closed manifold — rather than allowing wave-through continuation. This mechanism was identified in the physical picture document *SCH_GalacticEngine_PhysicalPicture_v1* as the previously-missing coupling between local astrophysical processes and the global $\omega(t)$ that drives the bounce cosmology, and as the mechanistic link that unifies four predictions previously treated as parallel but unconnected: the black hole bounce resonance (P.7.5), $S^3$ spatial topology (P.7.6), the antipodal CMB correlation signature (Paper B Section 7.2), and the bounce cosmology energy cycle (P.7.4).

*Required derivation.* The calculation has three sub-targets that must be addressed in sequence.

**Sub-target (a) — Mode coupling at the antipodal point.** Establish that the nonlinear $\lambda(\bar{\psi}\psi)^2$ term in $S_{\text{geo}}$ produces genuine mode coupling at the antipodal convergence point rather than linear wave-through propagation. This requires analyzing the condensate field equation on $S^3$ in the vicinity of the antipodal point, identifying the conditions under which the nonlinear term transfers energy from the incoming propagating mode into global eigenmode excitation, and demonstrating that this coupling is non-negligible for condensate wave amplitudes in the astrophysically relevant range. A sufficient result would show that above a threshold wave amplitude (set by the ratio $\alpha / \lambda$), the arriving wave excites global modes with nonzero coupling strength. A null result — no mode coupling at any amplitude — would require an alternative mechanism for the bounce cosmology energy cycle and would weaken the mechanistic connection between the four unified predictions.

**Sub-target (b) — Energy transfer rate.** Compute the energy transfer rate $\dot{E}_{\text{global}}$ from local condensate wave emission to global $S^3$ mode excitation as a function of wave amplitude $|\eta_{\text{wave}}|$, frequency $f_{\text{cond}}$, and the manifold radius $R_{\text{universe}}$. The rate should be expressible in terms of established framework parameters $\{\alpha, \lambda, m, m_{\text{eff}}, R_{\text{universe}}\}$ once the mode coupling structure of sub-target (a) is known. This rate is the input to the modified Friedmann equations (CT-viii) that describes how black hole emission replenishes $\omega(t)$ against rotational energy consumed by matter coherence across the cosmic expansion.

**Sub-target (c) — Energy cycle closure.** Show that the integrated contribution of all black holes in the observable $S^3$ volume — weighted by their mass function and summed over the redshift history of the universe — is sufficient to maintain $\omega(t)$ at levels consistent with the observed value of $c = \omega(t) \cdot R_{\text{cosmic}}(t)$ over the cosmic expansion timescale. This sub-target closes the bounce cosmology energy cycle: it establishes that the universe's black hole population is not merely a passive recipient of the global rotation rate but is an active contributor to its maintenance. Full closure requires CT-viii (modified Friedmann equations) as a simultaneous input.

*Prerequisites.* CT-vii (black hole condensate propagator — full perturbative mode analysis around a Schwarzschild background) and CT-viii (FLRW reduction and modified Friedmann equations on $S^3 \times \mathbb{R}$). CT-xix should not be attempted until both prerequisites are complete, because sub-target (b) requires the condensate propagator to compute the wave amplitude arriving at the antipodal point, and sub-target (c) requires the modified Friedmann equations to close the energy cycle.

*What CT-xix unlocks.*

— Formal establishment of the bounce cosmology energy cycle, replacing the current qualitative argument with a derivation.

— Mechanistic unification of the four parallel predictions: black hole bounce resonance, $S^3$ topology, antipodal CMB correlation, and bounce cosmology. Prior to CT-xix, these are derived from the same axioms but do not causally connect. After CT-xix, antipodal convergence is the explicit causal link.

— The CMB antipodal correlation (Paper B Section 7.2) acquires a mechanism: it is the observational imprint of condensate waves arriving at antipodal convergence points and coupling into global modes. The signal amplitude becomes predictable in terms of the black hole mass function rather than remaining parametric.

— The cosmological timescale for condensate wave transit across $S^3$ becomes quantitatively defined, enabling a prediction of the phase relationship between antipodal sky pixel pairs $T(\mathbf{n}) \times T(-\mathbf{n})$ that is currently stated only qualitatively.

— Foundation for CT-xx (thermodynamic consistency).

*Falsification conditions.* (i) If sub-target (a) returns zero mode coupling for all astrophysically relevant wave amplitudes, the antipodal convergence mechanism does not operate and an alternative energy cycle mechanism for the bounce cosmology must be identified. The four parallel predictions would remain valid but mechanistically disconnected. (ii) If sub-target (c) shows the integrated black hole contribution is insufficient to maintain $\omega(t)$ at observed levels by more than an order of magnitude, the galactic engine picture requires revision. (iii) If the coupling produces mode excitation at frequencies inconsistent with the observed CMB quadrupole and antipodal correlation signature, the $S^3$ topology identification is constrained.

*Priority.* CT-xix should be pursued after CT-vii and CT-viii are complete. Given that CT-viii is already identified as a prerequisite for PT-1 (chirality inversion across bounce) and CT-ix ($R_{\text{universe}}$ derivation), CT-xix joins a cluster of targets dependent on the same prerequisite. **CT-xx** [New in v9]. Thermodynamic consistency of the coherence-forcing mechanism; entropy accounting for the galactic engine; information-export consequence for the black hole information paradox.

*Physical background.* The galactic engine picture developed in *SCH_GalacticEngine_PhysicalPicture_v1* describes a supermassive black hole as a continuous rotational energy processor: infallen matter carrying w-spin is processed through the Planck density bounce (Term 3, P.7.5) and emitted as coherent condensate waves (Term 2, Theorem 4) that maintain rotational coherence in halo matter against thermal decoherence. This mechanism is thermodynamically non-trivial: coherence is low entropy, and a mechanism that maintains local low-entropy states against thermal disruption requires that the entropy cost be paid elsewhere. The cosmic laser analogy — the SMBH as pump paying the entropy cost for halo coherence — provides a physically sound qualitative framework, but a quantitative entropy accounting has not been performed. CT-xx specifies that accounting.

A further consequence is at stake. If the Planck density bounce converts infallen disordered matter into ordered coherent outgoing condensate waves, it performs a thermodynamically nontrivial information conversion: the w-spin state of infallen matter, compressed and apparently scrambled at the Planck density, exits the black hole as coherent condensate wave content rather than remaining trapped behind the event horizon or emerging as structureless thermal Hawking radiation. This would constitute a specific resolution of the black hole information paradox within the SCH framework. The claim is strong and requires extraordinary rigor. CT-xx is the formal prerequisite for any assertion along these lines.

*Required derivation.* The calculation has four sub-targets.

**Sub-target (a) — Entropy of condensate wave emission versus Hawking emission.** For a black hole of mass $M$ emitting both Hawking thermal radiation and condensate waves at rate $f_{\text{cond}} \sim M^{-2}$, compute the entropy flux in each channel per unit energy output. Standard Hawking radiation carries entropy at the maximum thermal rate for its temperature $T_{\text{Hawking}} = \hbar c^3 / (8\pi G M k_B)$. The condensate wave emission is ordered and coherent; its entropy per unit energy is lower than the thermal maximum. The quantity required is $\Delta S = S_{\text{Hawking}} - S_{\text{condensate}}$ per unit emission energy — the entropy "savings" of coherent emission relative to thermal emission. This is a positive quantity if the condensate emission is genuinely more ordered than Hawking radiation.

**Sub-target (b) — SMBH internal entropy accounting.** Show that the entropy difference $\Delta S$ from sub-target (a) is paid for by the SMBH's internal entropy increase. The Bekenstein-Hawking entropy $S_{BH} = A / (4 \ell_P^2)$ provides the black hole's entropy budget. As the SMBH processes infallen matter through the bounce mechanism, its internal entropy increases at a rate that must be shown to be at least $\Delta S$ per unit emission energy. This sub-target requires tracking the entropy of the infallen matter through the Planck density bounce — specifically, showing that the nonlinear Term 3 regime at Planck density is the step at which the conversion of disordered infallen matter into ordered outgoing waves occurs, and that this conversion is thermodynamically consistent with the second law when the SMBH's entropy increase is included.

**Sub-target (c) — Total entropy production rate.** Demonstrate that the total entropy production rate of the system — SMBH plus condensate field plus halo matter — is non-negative throughout the coherence-forcing cycle. Specifically: $\dot{S}_{\text{total}} = \dot{S}_{BH} + \dot{S}_{\text{condensate}} + \dot{S}_{\text{halo}} \geq 0$ at all times, where $\dot{S}_{\text{halo}}$ may be negative (reflecting local coherence maintenance) while $\dot{S}_{BH}$ is positive and large. A sufficient result demonstrates that $\dot{S}_{BH} \geq |\dot{S}_{\text{halo}}|$ for all astrophysically relevant configurations. Failure to demonstrate this would not necessarily falsify the coherence-forcing mechanism — it would require a re-examination of the entropy accounting rather than abandonment of the mechanism — but it would indicate that the cosmic laser analogy is quantitatively incorrect and that a different entropy source must be identified.

**Sub-target (d) — Information-export consequence (contingent).** If sub-targets (a) through (c) establish that the condensate wave emission carries ordered w-spin information about infallen matter at a rate and fidelity that accounts for the entropy difference, then formally characterize the information content of the condensate wave emission in terms of w-spin state encoding. The question to be answered is: to what degree does the condensate wave emitted after a bounce event carry recoverable information about the w-spin state of the infallen matter that triggered it? Three possible results are distinguished:

*Full encoding:* The condensate wave carries complete w-spin information about infallen matter, and the information paradox is resolved through coherent condensate export rather than Hawking correlations. This is the strongest possible result and requires showing that the Term 3 nonlinearity at Planck density performs a reversible (or nearly reversible) encoding rather than an irreversible scrambling.

*Partial encoding:* The condensate wave carries partial w-spin information — the large-scale rotational organizational state of the infallen matter but not its microscopic quantum state. The information paradox is partially resolved in the sense that macroscopic coherence information exits coherently, but microscopic quantum information is scrambled at the Planck density and exits only through Hawking correlations. This is the most physically plausible result given the extreme nonlinearity of the bounce.

*No encoding:* The Planck density bounce is effectively an irreversible scrambler: infallen w-spin information is lost at the bounce, and the outgoing condensate wave carries only the total energy and angular momentum of the bounce, not the w-spin organizational state of the infallen matter. In this case, the coherence-forcing mechanism is thermodynamically consistent (sub-targets a–c can still be satisfied through energy-based accounting) but the condensate emission is not a channel for information export. The information paradox remains unresolved within SCH.

Sub-target (d) is explicitly contingent on sub-targets (a) through (c) returning consistent results. It is not an independent claim of this document. It is stated here so that the implications of the calculation are clear before it is attempted. The result of sub-target (d) is designated exploratory until CT-xx sub-targets (a) through (c) are complete.

*Prerequisites.* CT-xix (antipodal condensate coupling) is a prerequisite for the full CT-xx programme, because the entropy cycle must be closed globally through the antipodal mechanism. However, sub-targets (a) and (b) are partially tractable before CT-xix is complete: the local entropy accounting for a single black hole's emission can be performed using only the Schwarzschild background and the condensate propagator (CT-vii). The full global closure (sub-target c) requires CT-xix. The Bi-209 calibration is required for quantitative magnitudes throughout but not for the structural entropy accounting in sub-targets (a) and (b), which can be expressed parametrically in $\alpha$ and $m_{\text{eff}}$.

*What CT-xx unlocks.*

— Formal thermodynamic status of the coherence-forcing mechanism, elevating the cosmic laser analogy from argued to established (or falsifying it with a specific thermodynamic failure mode identified).

— Quantitative prediction of the range over which a given SMBH can maintain halo coherence as a function of its mass, emission power, and the thermal decoherence rate of halo matter. This converts the M-sigma relation consequence (identified as exploratory in *SCH_GalacticEngine_PhysicalPicture_v1* Section 5) into a derivable result.

— The information-export consequence, contingent on sub-target (d) outcome: either a resolution mechanism for the black hole information paradox, or a precise characterization of the degree to which SCH's coherence-forcing mechanism is and is not an information-export channel.

— Foundation for quantitative predictions of rotation curve flattening radii (Prediction P-2 in *SCH_GalacticEngine_PhysicalPicture_v1*) and halo size as a function of SMBH mass.

*Falsification conditions.* (i) If sub-target (c) demonstrates $\dot{S}_{BH} < |\dot{S}_{\text{halo}}|$ for typical galaxy configurations, the second law is violated by the coherence-forcing mechanism as described, requiring either a revision of the entropy accounting or a modification of the galactic engine picture. (ii) If the entropy "savings" of coherent condensate emission relative to Hawking emission is shown to be negligible — i.e., if $\Delta S \approx 0$ per unit emission energy — then the condensate emission carries the same entropy as thermal emission, the information-export consequence is closed, and sub-target (d) returns the "no encoding" result by default. (iii) If the Bi-209 calibration returns parameters ($\alpha$, $m_{\text{eff}}$) that make the condensate emission power negligible relative to Hawking emission power for all astrophysical black holes, the galactic engine mechanism is thermodynamically irrelevant and the coherence-forcing picture requires revision.

*Priority.* CT-xx is a long-horizon calculational target. Sub-targets (a) and (b) can be pursued after CT-vii, in parallel with CT-xix. Sub-targets (c) and (d) follow after CT-xix is complete. The Bi-209 calibration is required before quantitative magnitudes can be computed. Suggested sequencing: CT-vii → {CT-xix, CT-xx(a,b) in parallel} → CT-xx(c,d) after CT-xix.

A note on scope: CT-xx engages with the black hole information paradox, which is one of the most contested problems in theoretical physics. The claim made here is limited: *if the condensate wave emission carries ordered information about infallen matter's w-spin state, and if the entropy accounting of sub-targets (a)–(c) is consistent, then a specific information-export mechanism exists within SCH.* This is a conditional claim with explicit prerequisites. It is not an assertion that SCH resolves the information paradox. It is an identification of the calculation that would determine whether SCH has anything to say about it.
