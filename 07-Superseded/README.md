# `07-Superseded` 

## Retired Material

Everything in this folder is **Tier 3**: retired, kept for history and
audit purposes, and **not citable as current theory under any
circumstance**. Nothing here is deleted when it's replaced — it's
moved here with the document that replaced it stating, in its own
revision history, what changed and why. If you're trying to understand
how the framework arrived at its current form, or you're checking
whether a specific claim you remember reading is still current, this is
where the previous versions live.

## Structure

**`top-level-superseded/`** — retired drafts of the core papers and
formal appendix, organized by which document they belong to
(`Paper_A/`, `Paper_B/`, `Paper_C/`, `Appendix_P/`), plus `misc/` for
anything that doesn't fit that pattern. A document here was, at some
point, the canonical statement of that paper — check the *current*
version's revision history (in `canonical/`) to see exactly what it
was replaced by and why.

**`working-superseded/`** — retired working papers, proof attempts, and
internal derivations that never were the canonical statement of
anything, but fed into one. This is where you'll find abandoned
derivation routes, earlier internal versions of documents that were
later folded wholesale into a canonical document, and governance
charter history.

## Why Wrong Derivations Are Kept, Not Deleted

Several documents in `working-superseded/` are here specifically
*because* they contained an error — not despite it. The project's own
discipline (see `governance/`) treats a caught error as valuable
history, not something to erase: knowing that a particular derivation
route was tried and failed, and specifically how, is part of what makes
the eventual confirmed result trustworthy. If a filename here looks
like it should contain the "real" answer to something, check its
opening note first — several of these documents say plainly, in their
own first paragraph, that they were later found to be wrong, and point
to what replaced them.

## The One Rule

Nothing in this folder is used as a citation, a dependency, or a
premise for any document in `canonical/` or `track-b/`. If a document
outside this folder needs something that's only stated here, that's a
sign the dependency map in `governance/` needs updating before, not
after, that citation is made.
